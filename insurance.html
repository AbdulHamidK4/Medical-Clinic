INSURANCE QUESTIONS

A little clarity
goes a long way.

What if my plan isn’t listed?+

The plan names above are reference content, not a verified acceptance list. Contact the clinic to confirm coverage for your particular plan and appointment.

Can I pay for care myself?+

Ask the clinic about self-pay availability and request a written estimate before your consultation. Prices are not provided in this preview.

Does an appointment request confirm coverage?+

No. An appointment request does not verify insurance coverage, authorisation or the cost of a visit.

Should I send my insurance documents through this site?+

No. Do not send policy numbers, medical records or other sensitive documents through this preview. Ask the clinic for its approved secure process.
